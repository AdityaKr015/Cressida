---
title: Inbox
description:
  Catch up on unread conversations, revisit mentions and save messages for
  later.
---

Open **Inbox** at the right of the chat header, or press **Ctrl+I**. It is also
available from Friends and Message Requests. Use the four tabs to catch up
without leaving the conversation you are reading.

Opening the inbox does not mark messages as read. Click a card to jump to its
conversation. Your current draft stays in that conversation.

## Unread

Unread shows conversations with unread messages, newest activity first. Each
card includes a short preview. Previews load as cards become visible.

Use the checkmark to mark that channel as read. This includes unread messages
beyond the preview. Use **Next** and **Previous** when there are more channels
than fit on one page.

Muted channels are hidden initially. Open **Inbox filters** to include them or
limit the list to the current server.

## Mentions

Mentions shows your recent account mentions across servers and conversations.
The checkmark dismisses that mention from the inbox without marking the entire
channel as read.

**Older** loads the next page and **Newest** returns to recent mentions. Filters
can limit results to the current server and include or exclude role mentions and
everyone mentions. Muted conversations are hidden until **Include muted** is
selected.

## Bookmarks

Open a message's menu and choose **Bookmark Message**. You can also use the
bookmark icon on an inbox card. Bookmarks are saved to your account and can be
viewed on other clients that support saved messages.

Click a bookmark to return to the original message. The remove button deletes
the bookmark, not the message. **Older** and **Newest** browse saved pages.

A deleted or inaccessible message may retain a saved summary. Opening it still
requires access to its original conversation.

## Reminders

Choose **Remind Me** from a message menu, or use the bell on an inbox card.
Choose **20 minutes**, **1 hour**, **3 hours** or **24 hours**.

Reminders are saved to your account. A reminder replaces the bookmark for that
message. To keep it as a bookmark instead, use its bookmark icon. Use the remove
button to complete a reminder.

While Cressida is running, newly due reminders can produce a desktop
notification. They respect your notification switch, sound setting, privacy
setting and Do Not Disturb status. Due reminders remain in this tab until you
remove them. Starting the client does not send a burst of notifications for
reminders that were already due.

Cressida must be running for its own reminder notifications to appear. Closing
the window to a supported tray host keeps it running.

## Empty lists and loading

Bookmarks and Reminders show an illustrated guide until you save your first
message. An account without a saved-message collection also starts with an empty
list.

Loading has its own screen. If a request fails, the tab shows one explanation
and a **Retry** button. An existing page stays visible when loading another page
fails.

## Keyboard and refresh

- **Ctrl+I** opens or closes the inbox.
- **Left / Right** changes tabs.
- **Up / Down** scrolls the list.
- **Page Up / Page Down** scrolls a page.
- **Escape** cancels reminder choices first, then closes the inbox.

The refresh button reloads the current tab. Live account events update saved
messages and dismissed mentions while connected. Loading and save errors stay
visible; a failed action keeps the original item so you can refresh and try
again.
